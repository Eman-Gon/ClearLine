    A CLEARER VIEW OF YOUR DATA

    From your voice to a next step.

    1. This device → paired laptop

      Your complete recording is sent after you stop. Voice processing and model inference happen on the laptop.

    2. Only with separate export consent → RawTree

      Approved derived measurements and workflow metadata. Never audio, transcripts, names, secrets, or full model messages.

    3. Only when you request it → Nimble

      Your selected public search category and city. Resource pages are evidence, and availability may remain unknown.

    This page keeps only session/clip identifiers and a resume token in browser storage. Audio stays in page memory while awaiting upload; it is lost if you close or reload before acceptance. The backend removes temporary media after processing no longer needs it; this is not a promise of forensic erasure.
